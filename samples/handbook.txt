Employee Handbook (Sample)

1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Employees may adopt flexible hours between 7:00 and 19:00 with their manager's approval, provided they are available for core hours from 10:00 to 15:00. Remote work is permitted up to three days per week for roles that do not require on-site presence. Employees working remotely must be reachable on the company chat during core hours.

2. Vacation Policy
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued monthly at 1.67 days per month. After five years of continuous service, the allowance increases to 25 days. Vacation requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three consecutive days. Up to five unused vacation days may be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on March 31. Unused days beyond that are forfeited and are not paid out, except where required by local law. Part-time employees receive vacation on a pro-rata basis.

3. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than three consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry over between years. Employees should notify their manager as early as possible on the first day of absence.

4. Public Holidays
The company observes all national public holidays. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is given as a day off. A list of observed holidays is published in the HR portal each December for the coming year.

5. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which may be taken within the first year after the birth or adoption. Employees must have completed six months of service to be eligible. Leave should be requested at least eight weeks in advance where possible.

6. Expenses
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days with itemised receipts. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 50 USD per day. Economy class is required for flights under six hours; premium economy may be booked for longer flights. Expenses above 500 USD require prior written approval from a department head.

7. Equipment
Each employee receives a laptop, which is refreshed every three years. Remote employees may claim a one-time home office allowance of 300 USD for a chair, desk, or monitor. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

8. Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Concerns may be raised with a manager, with HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.

9. Performance Reviews
Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Salary adjustments are considered during the December review and take effect on January 1. Employees set three to five goals with their manager at the start of each review cycle.

10. Leaving the Company
Employees are asked to give four weeks' notice of resignation. Accrued but unused vacation for the current year is paid out on departure. Company equipment must be returned on or before the last working day.
